01.
Opening Letter

The market we are looking at — Monmouth County luxury real estate — has never been served by an agent who is simultaneously a Christie's International affiliate, a bilingual native Spanish speaker, and an operator with genuinely editorial taste. Carolina Solorzano is that wedge. The opening is not theoretical: the $2M–$10M Rumson, Colts Neck, Holmdel, and Navesink corridor has zero top-producing agent branded in Spanish, and the sites that do dominate — Diane Turton, Heritage House, Resources, Glen Kelly — are functionally built for 2018. Our research found no competitor running scroll-driven cinematic video, no genuine /es locale, and no per-listing microsite treatment anywhere in the corridor. The halo Carolina already carries from Christie's and from the Teresa Minnick Group is resting on a templated profile page she does not own, does not control, and cannot optimize.

What we are proposing to build is a singular, self-owned editorial house: a cinematic, scroll-driven website anchored by a scroll-scrubbed drone sequence over the Navesink River at golden-twilight, with a secondary Pier Village reveal and a locked-in close on the Monmouth skyline. Beneath the hero: an understated Christie's International lockup, a curated portfolio (not an MLS firehose), pinned neighborhood films for Holmdel, Red Bank, Rumson, and Long Branch, and a bilingual editorial bio where Spanish is a first-class citizen — not a Google Translate afterthought. A concierge AI voice agent on a dedicated phone line handles bilingual inbound after hours and routes qualified leads to Carolina by SMS. Every page carries true /en and /es parity, schema-marked, Google-Business-Profile-indexed, and built to own the Spanish-speaking-realtor-NJ keyword cluster that no one is fighting for.

The reason to move now is simple, and it is the most underrated phrase in this entire document: there is no legacy SEO to preserve. Carolina does not rank for any competitive Monmouth County query today, which means a new domain built from day one around her name, her bilingual positioning, and her Christie's affiliation can be architected clean. Her competitors carry 15–30 years of templated infrastructure; she carries a blank page. Inside a year we build her the editorial house, the bilingual content moat, the voice-agent-and-SMS funnel, and the per-listing film framework. It is the rare moment where greenfield positioning and an incumbent-grade brand halo arrive in the same six months.

V.  Hero Animation Concept

Four beats over the Navesink.

A scroll-scrubbed cinematic opens the site — not a looping background video, but a genuinely scroll-responsive sequence where the user pilots the drone. Shot at golden-twilight, graded warm, rendered in AVIF for speed.

01
Slow boom out from a wood-shingle gable.

We open close — the grain of a cedar-shake gable in first light, shot tight enough to feel private. As the user makes their first scroll gesture, the camera begins a slow vertical boom, pulling away from the roofline. The wordmark CAROLINA SOLORZANO is not yet visible. The chrome is cool, the light is low-angle, the mood is intimate.

02
Wide aerial crossing the river toward Sandy Hook.

The boom resolves into a wide tracking shot — the drone crosses the Navesink, water-light flickering underneath, the far bank of Rumson revealing itself frame by frame. The serif wordmark fades in at 30% scroll, executing a 400ms weight-up reveal from regular to medium. The nav bar transitions from transparent to navy-deep at 15%.

03
Golden-hour chromatic shift.

Mid-sequence the grade pivots warm — cool morning cedes to honeyed twilight across the sequence, champagne gold bleeding into the water. A subtle parallax pass on foreground reeds adds depth without noise. A 4% film-grain overlay holds the whole frame as analog rather than stock. This is the second the Jersey Shore reads as Atlantic luxury, not summer-rental.

04
Lock on Monmouth skyline, Private Showing fades in.

The sequence lands — a held wide of the Monmouth skyline at blue-hour, Pier Village distantly glowing. The primary CTA, set in small caps, Private Showing, fades in with a magnetic underline. A secondary line, “Arrange a private conversation · EN / ES,” sets the bilingual signal from the first interaction. The scroll-scrub resolves. The film is done; the site begins.

“The buyer has not searched for a listing yet. She has already agreed to let the drone keep flying.”

VII.  Technical Approach

Vanilla, precise, fast.

No framework bloat, no CMS overhead, no client-rendered JavaScript drag on the first paint. The site is hand-built, scroll-first, and deployed on a global edge with a full EN/ES route tree.

Vanilla HTML / CSS / JavaScript. No React, no Next, no build-step bloat. Zero client-rendering penalty on first paint. Hand-tuned for the fastest possible hero load.
GSAP 3.12.5 scroll engine. ScrollTrigger drives the drone frame sequence, scroll-pinned neighborhood films, magnetic CTAs, and split-scroll bilingual bio.
Google Fonts self-hosted fallback. Fraunces (variable, with opsz axis), Inter, and Cormorant Garamond — pre-loaded, subsetted, with fallback system stack.
Vercel deployment. Global edge network, custom domain, automatic HTTPS/SSL, preview URL per branch, zero-config CDN.
GitHub auto-deploy on push. Connected repository; every commit to main ships to production. Rollback is one click, history is searchable.
Schema.org RealEstateAgent structured data. Full JSON-LD markup for your name, brokerage affiliation, service area, languages spoken, offices, license — indexed as a rich snippet.
Open Graph & Twitter Cards. Custom preview image per page — the editorial cover renders cleanly in iMessage, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and every social share surface.
Inline SVG favicon from brand mark. Monogram C / S lockup in navy on ivory, rendered as inline SVG — sharp on retina, dark-mode aware, zero-request.
Full /en and /es route parity. Genuine bilingual routing — every page has a canonical Spanish counterpart, hreflang-tagged, with independent SEO surfaces.
Google Business Profile integration. Claimed, verified, linked to the site's schema markup; review funnel connected to the concierge form so happy clients are routed in.
