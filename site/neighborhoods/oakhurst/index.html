THE OVERVIEW

The Wooded Side of the Shore.

Where younger families from Brooklyn and Jersey City arrive for the yard, the schools, and the ten-minute drive to the Atlantic Ocean.

Oakhurst is the inland, woody half of Ocean Township. Streets like West Park Avenue, Roseld, and Norwood Drive are lined with restored Tudor Revivals, Dutch Colonials, and mid-century Ranches on quarter- to half-acre wooded lots. The original Jewish and Italian communities who built out this area in the 1940s-1960s have given way to younger families — many arriving from Brooklyn, Jersey City, and Manhattan for the yard and the schools.

Median sits near $750K. The Ocean Township School District is strong, and Oakhurst's proximity to Route 35 (seven minutes) and the Asbury Park station (nine minutes) makes it a realistic option for NYC commuters who aren't ready for the Rumson price tag.

Mirtha represents the inland-shore buyer — someone who wants the atmosphere of the Jersey Shore region without being on the ocean, and the diverse Hispanic community that has grown quickly in the last decade.

LIFESTYLE

What You'll Find Here.

Ocean Township Schools

The shared K-12 district — Ocean Township High plus feeder elementaries, with strong academics and a diverse student body.

Jumping Brook Country Club

The private golf and tennis club on Jumping Brook Road — the social anchor for many Oakhurst families and a quiet weekend center.

Norwood Drive

One of Oakhurst's signature canopied streets — Tudors, Dutch Colonials, and deep setbacks under oak and maple cover.

West Park Avenue

The residential spine connecting Oakhurst to West Allenhurst and Deal — estate homes and the most consistent tree canopy in Ocean Township.

Poplar Brook Reservation

The wooded natural area cutting through the neighborhood — walking paths, native hardwoods, and the reason Oakhurst feels like the forested shore.

Monmouth Mall (redeveloping)

The adjacent retail anchor being redeveloped into a mixed-use town center — a quiet accelerator for Oakhurst property values.

NJ Transit Asbury Park Station

A nine-minute drive away — the commuter rail link to Penn Station that makes Oakhurst realistic for NYC professionals.

Monmouth Medical Center

The regional hospital on the Long Branch border — the medical anchor for Oakhurst families and a major county employer.
