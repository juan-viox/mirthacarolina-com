THE OVERVIEW

Historic Main Street, Modern Commute.

The historic half of the Matawan-Aberdeen pairing — restored brick storefronts, Victorian side streets, and the same 53-minute rail to Penn Station.

Matawan is the historic half of the Matawan-Aberdeen pairing. Main Street has been restored with boutique retail, farm-to-table restaurants (Catch + Gather, Fiddleheads), and the Matawan Community Theatre — all in 19th-century brick storefronts that reference the town's ship-building history. The residential side streets are walkable Victorians and Queen Anne cottages.

Median sits near $525K in the historic Matawan borough section and climbs into the $1M+ range for the older homes on Broad Street and Main Street. The Matawan-Aberdeen Regional School District serves here, and the Aberdeen-Matawan NJ Transit station puts Penn Station 53 minutes away.

Mirtha represents a current rental listing at 114 Wellington Place in Matawan, and she works with the many commuter families who choose Matawan for the walkable historic character plus the fast rail access.

LIFESTYLE

What You'll Find Here.

Main Street Historic District

The restored 19th-century brick storefront corridor — boutique retail, restaurants, and the most intact small-town main street in northwestern Monmouth.

Matawan Community Theatre

The restored 1928 movie house — now a community theater hosting plays, concerts, and the borough's cultural calendar.

NJ Transit Aberdeen-Matawan Station

The shared commuter rail stop — 53-minute direct ride to Penn Station and the anchor of both Matawan and Aberdeen's property values.

Matawan Creek

The tidal creek winding through the borough — the historic ship-building waterway and a quiet natural feature at the town's edge.

Terhune Park

The borough's central green space — playground, tennis, and the Saturday-morning gathering point for Matawan families.

Matawan-Aberdeen Regional Schools

The shared K-12 district — a diverse, commuter-oriented public school anchor for both boroughs.

Broad Street

The older residential spine — Victorians, Queen Annes, and American Foursquares walkable from the Main Street boutiques.

Farm-to-Table Dining

Catch + Gather, Fiddleheads, and a growing crop of locally-sourced restaurants — Matawan is quietly becoming one of the county's food destinations.
