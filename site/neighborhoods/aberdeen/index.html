THE OVERVIEW

The Commuter's Gateway to Monmouth.

Where Monmouth County starts as you come south from New York — the fastest rail to Penn Station in the county, with two active Mirtha listings anchoring the market.

Aberdeen is where Monmouth County starts as you come south from New York. The Aberdeen-Matawan NJ Transit station sits on the North Jersey Coast Line, with direct service to Penn Station in 53 minutes — which makes Aberdeen one of the fastest commuter options in the county. Residential streets cluster around Cliffwood Beach (waterfront access on Raritan Bay), Cliffwood, Strathmore, and Freneau.

Housing ranges from $450K Cape Cods and split-levels to $2M+ waterfront Cliffwood Beach homes. Median sits near $575K in the main residential sections and $1.2M+ for bay-view or private-lot estates. The Matawan-Aberdeen Regional School District serves both boroughs, and the Garden State Parkway Exit 117 is two minutes away.

Mirtha's work in Aberdeen is active right now — two listings (55 Lower Main Street, residential, and 537 South Atlantic Avenue, luxury residential) currently represent her in this market. Bilingual representation is particularly valuable in Aberdeen's growing Hispanic community.

LIFESTYLE

What You'll Find Here.

NJ Transit Aberdeen-Matawan Station

The North Jersey Coast Line stop — 53-minute direct ride to Penn Station, the fastest rail commute in Monmouth County.

Matawan-Aberdeen Regional Schools

The shared K-12 district — a diverse, commuter-oriented public school anchor serving both boroughs.

Cliffwood Beach

The bayfront neighborhood on Raritan Bay — waterfront homes, a small beach, and views across the bay toward Staten Island.

Garden State Parkway

The Exit 117 interchange — two minutes from most of the township and the rapid highway link to the rest of Monmouth County and NYC.

Henry Hudson Trail

The multi-use rail-trail threading through Aberdeen — running toward Atlantic Highlands for cyclists and runners.

Aberdeen Municipal Pool

The summer-season recreation anchor — a gathering point for township families and one of the county's well-run public pools.

Cliffwood Lake

The small freshwater lake in the bayfront neighborhood — a quieter alternative to the Raritan Bay for paddling and family walks.

Raritan Bay Waterfront

The northern edge of the township — bay views, private docks for Cliffwood Beach homes, and the fishing and crabbing scene.
