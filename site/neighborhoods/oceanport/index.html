THE OVERVIEW

Horse Country on the Shrewsbury.

Where Haskell Stakes summer crowds meet a quiet river borough — new Fort Monmouth inventory, restored Tudors, and one of the cleanest commutes in the county.

Oceanport is defined by two things: Monmouth Park Racetrack (summer thoroughbred racing, Haskell Stakes) and its position on the Shrewsbury River. The rest is a quiet borough of restored Colonials, Tudors, and mid-century ranches on half-acre to one-acre lots. Parts of the old Fort Monmouth are being redeveloped into new residential communities, adding new-construction inventory to a historically stable market.

Median sits near $1.1M, with Shrewsbury River-facing properties pushing $2M+. The Shore Regional High School district (shared with Monmouth Beach, Sea Bright, Long Branch, and West Long Branch) is strong, and Oceanport's NJ Transit station puts Penn Station 68 minutes away.

LIFESTYLE

What You'll Find Here.

Monmouth Park Racetrack

The summer thoroughbred track — Haskell Stakes weekend draws international racing crowds and anchors Oceanport's summer economy.

Shrewsbury River

The tidal waterway defining the borough's southern edge — with private docks, waterfront estates, and direct boat access to Sandy Hook Bay.

Fort Monmouth Redevelopment

The decommissioned Army post being redeveloped into residential, commercial, and public-use parcels — the single largest new-inventory source in Oceanport's future.

NJ Transit Little Silver / Monmouth Park Stations

Two stops on the North Jersey Coast Line — a 68-minute direct commute to Penn Station and the rail spine of the county.

Shore Regional High

The shared high school district with Monmouth Beach, Sea Bright, Long Branch, and West Long Branch — a strong regional public-school anchor.

Wolf Hill Recreation

The borough's rec complex — fields, a pool, and the family-calendar center for Oceanport's year-round residents.

Oceanport Landing Marina

The private-slip marina on the Shrewsbury — home to sailing culture, summer rafting-up, and the river-access that defines borough life.

Haskell Stakes Summer Scene

The racing weekend that fills every restaurant and rental — a Kentucky Derby-adjacent event that briefly turns Oceanport into an international scene.
