A bayfront borough perched on the hillside — Sandy Hook Bay, Manhattan skyline views, and the fastest ferry to New York City.

THE OVERVIEW

The Highest Point on the Atlantic Coast.

Where the Seastreak ferry gets to Wall Street in 35 minutes and nearly every hillside street has a Manhattan-skyline view.

Atlantic Highlands is unique on the Jersey Shore for one reason: the elevation. The town is built up a steep hillside overlooking Sandy Hook Bay, which means nearly every street has a Manhattan-skyline view. The Seastreak ferry from Atlantic Highlands Municipal Marina gets to Wall Street in 35 minutes — faster than any New Jersey rail option.

Homes here are restored Victorian and Craftsman hillside mansions with broad front porches, plus newer construction on the elevated streets. Median sits near $650K on the inland streets and $1.5M+ for bay-view and Manhattan-view properties. The Henry Hudson Regional High School district serves here.

Mirtha represents the Manhattan commuter who wants the ferry lifestyle, the bayfront second-home buyer, and the growing arts-and-dining crowd that has made First Avenue one of the most interesting restaurant streets in Monmouth County.

LIFESTYLE

What You'll Find Here.

Seastreak Ferry to Wall Street

The 35-minute high-speed ferry from Atlantic Highlands Marina to Pier 11 in Lower Manhattan — the fastest commute in New Jersey.

Sandy Hook Bay

The protected bay defining the borough's northern edge — sailing, fishing, and the sunset view across to Staten Island.

First Avenue Dining

The independent-restaurant spine — Italian, seafood, and a rotating arts-community calendar that has made First Avenue a Monmouth County destination.

Henry Hudson Trail

The multi-use trail along the former rail corridor — running from Atlantic Highlands inland through Monmouth County, popular for cycling and running.

Atlantic Highlands Municipal Marina

The public marina serving the ferry and pleasure boating — the largest municipal marina in New Jersey and the civic center of borough life.

Mount Mitchill Scenic Overlook

The highest natural point on the eastern seaboard south of Maine — a Sandy Hook Bay overlook with direct Manhattan-skyline views.

Atlantic Highlands Historic District

The hillside blocks of restored Victorian cottages and Craftsman mansions — one of the most intact 1890s-1920s streetscapes in the county.

Henry Hudson Regional High

The small shared high school serving Atlantic Highlands and Highlands — strong community engagement and a waterfront-town cohort.
