A BILINGUAL EDITORIAL BIO

Two languages. One standard of service.

CHAPTER 01

From Diplomacy to the Atlantic.

Before real estate, Mirtha Carolina moved through diplomacy, international education, and brand marketing across three continents.

She chose Monmouth because it was the one American stretch of coast that felt both editorial and familial — the gable rooflines of Rumson, the hushed estate drives of Holmdel, the Atlantic pressed against Pier Village glass. Real estate, she decided, would be the medium that let her translate this place to buyers who had not yet found it.

CHAPTER 02

Two Languages. One Standard.

Spanish is not a service here — it is a first language, held to the same editorial standard as the English.

For clients arriving from Bogotá, Madrid, Mexico City, or Buenos Aires, Mirtha Carolina offers full bilingual parity: every contract, every private showing, every market note read and written in the language you think in. Nothing is translated twice.

CHAPTER 03

Anchored in Christie's. Rooted in Monmouth.

Backed by the Christie's International Real Estate network and the Teresa Minnick Group — over eight hundred million in career team sales.

The Christie's brand opens a global front door; Monmouth gives it a local address. Working from the Holmdel office at 59 Main Street, Mirtha pairs the auction-house reach of Christie's with the on-the-ground knowledge of a realtor who knows which Rumson driveway is worth walking up and which Holmdel estate is about to come off-market.
