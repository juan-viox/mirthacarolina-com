Signature Moment

The cinematic hero. Piloted by the scroll.

A custom drone sequence — slow boom-out from a Navesink River waterfront estate at golden twilight. Generated, animated, and scroll-scrubbed. The user does not watch the film. They fly it.

What it is

A canvas-rendered drone film.

A four-beat sequence that reveals Monmouth as the user moves through the page — cedar gable, aerial, golden-hour shift, skyline lock with CTA fade.

How it works

Frames tied to scroll position.

GSAP ScrollTrigger drives a canvas that paints the correct frame for every scroll offset. Frame 1 is preloaded for instant paint. Reduced-motion is honored.

The stack

Gemini → Kling → ffmpeg → GSAP.

Gemini 3.1 Flash generates the 2K still. WaveSpeed Kling v3 Pro animates to 5s video. ffmpeg extracts 121 frames. GSAP paints them on scroll.

Technical Hardening

SEO · Accessibility · Core Web Vitals.

Everything Google rewards and every audit score — built into the template layer, not bolted on at the end.

  1. Bilingual parity1,700+ paired data-en / data-es attributes across the site — editorial Spanish, hand-tuned, not machine-translated.
  2. Hreflangen + es + x-default declared on every page.
  3. Open Graph + TwitterPer-page cards with 1200×630 imagery and Twitter handle linked.

  6. Robots.txtSitemap pointer and explicit AI crawler policy.
  7. Custom 404Bilingual, with noindex for crawl hygiene.
  8. Resource hintspreload hero frame 1 · dns-prefetch · preconnect Google Fonts.
  9. Core Web VitalsExplicit width/height on every image, below-fold lazy-loading, CLS prevented.

09 · One of One

Eight load-bearing decisions.

This is not a template. It is not a theme. It is not configurable by a non-technical user — and that is the point. Every decision was load-bearing.

  1. The Navesink drone hero.Not stock footage. A custom scene generated specifically to match Mirtha's geographic specialty.
  2. Twenty-two hand-tuned narratives.Neighborhood pages are not auto-generated. Each is editorially calibrated to the town it describes.
  3. Editorial Spanish.Hand-drafted. Not machine-translated. The tone shifts when Spanish readers arrive.
  4. Heritage Atlantic Broker. Modern Cinematic Eye.The positioning came out of a specific competitive finding — not a brainstorm.
  5. Christie's Red used surgically.Exactly three moments per page — affiliate lockup, hover underline, a single stat. Never decoratively.
  6. Champagne Gold rules instead of shadows.An intentional anti-SaaS decision. Rules where a template would reach for drop shadows.
  7. Magnetic cursor on "Private Showing."A cursor-follow specific to the editorial-luxury voice. Not a generic hover state.
  8. Seven voice-agent tools.Jessica does not answer questions. She gives guided tours.

A template site has no soul. A cinematic design-studio build has a specific one.
