## Phase 3 · Verification (varies — 1 to 14 days)

Google will offer one or more verification methods. **Don't skip this** — unverified profiles don't show up in search/maps.

| Method | Time | When offered |
|---|---|---|
| **Video verification** | ~5 days | Most common in 2026; you record a 1-min video walking through your office showing signage, the address number, and a recent piece of mail/business card. |
| **Postcard** | 5–14 days | Falling out of favor; Google sends a postcard with a 5-digit code to the address. |
| **Phone/text** | Instant | Sometimes offered for established brokerages; you'll get a 6-digit code. |
| **Email** | Instant | Rare; only for verified Google Workspace domains. |

If video is offered, do it immediately — fastest path. Have ready:
- Christie's signage at the office
- Mirtha's business card
- A recent piece of mail (insurance, license, etc.) showing the address

## Phase 6 · Review Funnel (ongoing — single biggest local-SEO factor)

Google ranks businesses with **more recent reviews** higher in the local pack. Target: **10+ reviews in 90 days**, then 2–3 new reviews/month forever.

### 6.1 Get the review link
Once verified, your Business Profile has a review URL. Find it under "Get more reviews" → "Share review form". The link looks like:
`https://g.page/r/CXXXXXXXXX/review`

Save it. This is your most valuable URL.

### 6.3 Review responses — respond to EVERY review
- 5-star: warm thank-you, mention the property/neighborhood (signals to Google that the review is authentic)
- 4-star: thank + ask what would have made it 5
- 1–3 star: acknowledge calmly, offer to discuss offline (never argue publicly). If review is fake, flag it via the GBP dashboard
